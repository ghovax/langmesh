What stands in the way, and what would clear it. Required when the standing is
`blocked`. When standing is not `blocked`, omit this field (`null`); the standing code
is what says it does not apply.

Say what the obstacle actually is — a credential nobody here holds, a service that is
down, a decision only the person can make, a permission that was refused — and say what
the person would have to do about it. An obstacle you cannot name that precisely is not
one you have established.

Say also which routes were tried and why each is closed, since the whole claim is that
none is open.
